Help & support

Sky Tonight · Jason Scott

Updated 21 October 2026

Choose your observing place

Open Settings to select a saved place, use optional device location, find a city, or enter coordinates and a time zone manually. Perth is the initial default, not a guess at your location. Use Edit places beside Saved places to remove places.

Weather is optional

Enable Include Apple Weather in Settings to add conditions. Without it, all astronomy features still work. If weather cannot refresh, check your internet connection and try again. Old or missing conditions are shown as unavailable; they are not a guarantee of visibility or safety.

Why no ISS pass?

Only sunlit ISS passes in a dark sky, reaching at least 20 degrees and visible for at least one minute, are recommended. There may be no qualifying pass on a given night. Pull to refresh; expired orbital elements are not used for reliable predictions. Clouds and local obstructions can still prevent observing.

Preview and compass

Tap a recommendation or Show me where to open its viewing time in the Sky tab. Use Return to Live to return to the current sky. Drag to rotate, or enable the compass on a supported device. Keep away from magnetic interference; compass availability varies by device.

Home and Lock Screen widgets

Touch and hold the Home Screen, choose Edit, Add Widget, then search for Sky Tonight. Edit a widget to choose a saved place. Add Lock Screen widgets in Lock Screen customization where supported. Apple controls refresh timing, so widgets and background refreshes are not precise timers.

Reminders and privacy

Use Remind me on a future event and grant notification permission. Your system Focus and notification settings affect delivery. Review permissions in device Settings. Use Sky Tonight Settings, Clear saved data to reset local places, forecasts and reminders.
